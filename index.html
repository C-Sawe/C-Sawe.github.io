About

I'm Caleb Kipchirchir. On GitHub I'm C-Sawe. I'm a final-year Informatics and Computer Science student at Strathmore University in Nairobi, and I work on the gap between a model that runs in a notebook and a system that keeps running in production.

What I do

  • Applied reinforcement learning and MLOps: PPO agents, drift detection and automated retraining.
  • Automation and AI agents: WhatsApp agents on language models, and n8n workflows.
  • Back ends in Python (FastAPI, Flask), PHP (Laravel) and Node, on PostgreSQL and MySQL.
  • Front ends in React and Vue, with deck.gl or Three.js when the data is spatial.

Outside the degree

  • I run Mosop Farm Inputs, a farm supply business in Uasin Gishu with a branch in Nandi Hills, and I write its software.
  • I founded Nexatech, which builds WhatsApp automation for lenders, SACCOs and small businesses.

What in this room is automated

  • The robot on the desk answers questions about the shelf with an AI model, and pulls out the books it cites.
  • The tall monitor replays an example run of two things I build: a WhatsApp agent and a retraining loop. It is not live data.
  • The window follows your local time: a day sky by day, tonight's moon phase after dark.
  • The robot vacuum does its rounds on its own, usually with the cat on board.

How this page is designed

  • Nothing is invented. Every figure comes from a repository, and a book without screenshots says so.
  • Green and orange mean status only, and every status also has a text label.
  • System font, tabular numerals, one small type scale.
  • Motion is spring-based and switches off under reduced motion.
  • The three scrolls are original ink studies drawn in code: the Kenyan flag over an acacia, a run of candlesticks and a mountain at sunrise.
